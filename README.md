# One Letter for Your Day

Entry for the make.fun bounty [One Letter for Your Day](https://make.fun/bounty/u-puan-one-letter-for-your-day-e54e9c36).

## What it does

The program writes exactly one letter to `/output/entry.txt` — nothing else.
No word, no gloss, no punctuation, no trailing newline. Exactly one byte.

## The chosen letter: C

**C stands for chaos.**

The day was pure chaos: everything happening at once, nothing going to
plan, messages piling up, plans colliding — and somehow still funny to
tell afterwards.

Why C wins on the judge's brief, in the brief's own order:

1. **It stands for a real day-word.** Chaos is a word that describes a
   day, not a mood in search of a word.
2. **Vivid and specific.** Chaos calls up a sharp picture at once — the
   juggling, the collisions, the mess — rather than a vague mood like
   "amazing" or "great".
3. **Funny, and it lands at once.** "Today was pure chaos" is a line a
   reader repeats to a friend. It is self-deprecating, hyperbolic in
   exactly the way real people describe their days, and it needs no
   explanation.
4. **True to a real day.** Chaotic days are ordinary, universal, and
   honest — the letter reads as a report, not a performance. Everyone
   has had a chaos day; nobody has to pretend.
5. **The letter suits the word.** C is chaos's natural initial, and the
   hard "ch" sound even feels like the day it describes. And even if
   the reader hears "crazy" or "catastrophe" behind the C instead,
   every one of those is a strong day-word too — the pick is robust.

C is also deliberately none of the sponsor's named examples
(A for amazing, F for Friday-feeling, Z for zero sleep): those are the
most obvious letters, and the obvious letters are the crowded ones.

## Running it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the single letter `C` to `/output/entry.txt` (creating `/output`
if needed). Deterministic: the same byte on every run. Python 3.13,
standard library only, no network, no randomness, no clock.
